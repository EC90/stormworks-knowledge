# Gameplay/Mechanics/Mechanical Power

## Mechanical Power

Mechanical power is used to move vehicles.

It can be created by different [engines](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion "Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion"), such as prefab or modular engines, and consumed by [wheels](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Vehicle_Control "Wiki/Building/Components/Vehicle Control") or [propellers or rotors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion "Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion"). It can also be consumed by [generators](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Electricity "Wiki/Building/Components/Electricity") to produce [electrical power](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Electricity "Wiki/Game Mechanics/Electricity").
