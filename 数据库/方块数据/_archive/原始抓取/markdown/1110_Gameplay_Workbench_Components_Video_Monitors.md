# Gameplay/Workbench/Components/Video/Monitors

## Monitors

Monitors show data from **Logic Video Connections**. This can be either from a [camera](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Video/Cameras "Wiki/Building/Components/Video/Cameras"), or a [Lua script](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Lua "Wiki/Game Mechanics/Lua").
Monitors can be mirrored, which also mirrors the video signal!
