# V1.13.5

**V1.13.5** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on February 12th, 2025.

## V1.13.5

- Feature - [#2818](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/2818) Seat helmet HUD overlay
- Feature - Stabilized camera pitch and yaw composite values
- Fix - [#27099](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/27099) Viewing scope underwater shading effects
- Fix - [#26571](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/26571) Initial world autosave time of day incorrect
- Fix - [#26574](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/26574) Underwater propellers applying relative fluid force in wrong direction
- Fix - [#26570](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/26570) Jet engine turbines now explode correctly when exceeding their max rps
- Fix - [#26570](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/26570) Damage meshes are now rebuilt correctly for all components with self damage mechanics
