## Airship

Airships are typically forgotten about in stormworks and a very uncommon thing to find in the workshop. They are typically overshadowed by helicopters. Airships can be useful however. They can hover in one spot like a helicopter while being way bigger and there is no danger of dying to rotor blades. Airships are very expensive usually and are not always a good option for career.
