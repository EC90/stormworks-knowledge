## Standard: **Truck**

Standard

LT1

[Create your badge here](https://sws.flaffipony.rocks?s=LT1%20%20)

This standard is meant for trucks and trailers.

### Rules of this standard

- [Parent standard rules apply](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land)
- Pilot seat (either WS for throttle/brake and AD for steering OR use arrow UP DOWN for throttle/brake and arrow LEFT RIGHT for steering) **THIS RULE APPLIES ONLY TO THE TRUCK, NOT THE TRAILER**
- Brake light (bright red light at the rear of the vehicle, can be the same light blocks used for normal rear light)
- Headlights (small or large spotlights) **THIS RULE APPLIES ONLY TO THE TRUCK, NOT THE TRAILER**

### Feature rules

- see [global feature rules](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Features)
- c this vehicle has 1 **horizontal** electrical connector to connect it with trailers. Position of the block depends on the size (see below)
- t this vehicle has 1 **vertical** electrical connector to connect it with trailers. Position of the block depends on the size (see below)

### Size rules

*The size does not influence the size of your vehicle! It just tells other people on which height your connector is.*

**Vertical connectors:**

- S Vertical connector height: 3 (Truck), 5 (Trailer)
- M Vertical connector height: 4 (Truck), 6 (Trailer)
- L Vertical connector height: 5 (Truck), 7 (Trailer)
- XL Vertical connector height: 6 (Truck), 8 (Trailer)
- XXL Vertical connector height: 7 (Truck), 9 (Trailer)

[![Vertical Connectors](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/3/38/Standard_Truck_Vertical_Connectors.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/500?cb=20190519195534)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/3/38/Standard_Truck_Vertical_Connectors.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190519195534)

**Horizontal connectors:**

- S Horizontal connector height: 2
- M Horizontal connector height: 3
- L Horizontal connector height: 4
- XL Horizontal connector height: 5
- XXL Horizontal connector height: 6
