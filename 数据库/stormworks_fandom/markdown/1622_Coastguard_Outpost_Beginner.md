A small island with a small boathouse allowing for the creation of a small boat or submarine. It will always spawn in a pair with the St. Sebastiaz Hospital island.

"Features a small dock." Stormworks Base Description

## Features

The site can be purchased for $10,000, granting access to its a harbor workbench for editing small ships of up to *. It also comes with 1 Tractor and 1 trailer that can be driven but cannot be taken into the workbench. It also comes with an overhead crane. It has a Diesel Fuel, Jet Fuel, and Electricity anchors, alongside coal storage. You are able to turn on and off the lights, as well as shut the shutters at the back of the Green building. The island also has 1 bed and a wardrobe in the white house up the hill. Loot crates can spawn on this island.

This base does not spawn any trailers or containers to transport.

The Coastguard Outpost Beginner base is also where the game will start the tutorial, regardless if you start there or not, entering the White building will begin the tutorial. Starting the tutorial spawns in a boat in the green building. It does not take fuel or money from the base/player. This boat can be taken into the workbench, but will not yield you any money or fuel.
