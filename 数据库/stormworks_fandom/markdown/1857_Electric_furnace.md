## Mechanics

Electric furnaces heat fluid using electricity. The fluid can reach a maximum temperature of ~372 inside the furnace when no fluid is moving through it.

The heat generation of the furnace slows down the higher the temperature is

Electric furnaces also produce ambient heat.
