## Cameras

Cameras produce a low resolution video which can be shown on [monitors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Video/Monitors).

[Camera Stabilized](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Camera_stabilized)

[![Camera with Gimbal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/1/1c/Camera_with_Gimbal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20200129203013)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/1/1c/Camera_with_Gimbal.png/revision/latest?cb=20200129203013)

Description: A stabilized gimbal camera with video output feed. Has an infrared mode, variable field of view, stabilized and tracking modes. It will only track terrain, and cannot lock onto vehicles or water.

- you can control rotation (yaw and pitch)
- you can control fov (zoom)
- you can control infrared
- you can enable/disable laser
- you can enable/disable stabilization
- you can enable/disable target tracking

Outputs:

- Video
- composite

Composite values:

- Number 1: coordinate x of laser target
- Number 2: altitude of laser target
- Number 3: coordinate y of laser target
- Bool 1: laser has target

[Camera with Gimbal](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Camera_gimbal)

[![Camera with Gimbal](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/1/1c/Camera_with_Gimbal.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/100?cb=20200129203013)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/1/1c/Camera_with_Gimbal.png/revision/latest?cb=20200129203013)

Description: A gimbal camera with video output feed. Has an infrared mode as well as pivot controls, [and] a variable field of view.

- you can control rotation (yaw and pitch)
- you can control fov (zoom)
- you can control infrared

Outputs:

- Video
