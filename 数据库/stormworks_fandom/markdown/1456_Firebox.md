# Firebox

## Firebox

A firebox is a component that produces heat by burning or using a certain resource. There are currently 4 in the game.

### Firebox Types

|  | Firebox | Firebox Large | Industrial Diesel Furnace | Electric Furnace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Size | 5x3x3 | 7x5x5 | 7x5x5 | 5x3x3 |
| Mass | 100 | 400 | 350 | 220 |
| Fuel | Coal | Coal | Diesel | Electric |
| Connections | Coolant In Coolant Out Air Exhaust | Coolant In Coolant Out Air Exhaust | Coolant In Coolant Out Air Exhaust | Coolant In Coolant out |
