# V0.9.9-10

**V0.9.9-10** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on November 8th, 2019.

## V0.9.9-10

- Feature - Playlist Selector

In the new game screen, you can now enable and disable playlists, including custom mission playlists. These playlists will now spawn in both career and creative. All playlists are enabled by default, so make sure to check if you are subscribed to many workshop items!

- Fix - Wheels flipping vehicles at speed when placed too close together
