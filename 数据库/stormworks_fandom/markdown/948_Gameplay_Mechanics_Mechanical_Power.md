## Mechanical Power

Mechanical power is used to move vehicles.

It can be created by different [engines](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion), such as prefab or modular engines, and consumed by [wheels](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Vehicle_Control) or [propellers or rotors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion). It can also be consumed by [generators](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Electricity) to produce [electrical power](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Electricity).
