# Status Effects

(this is not a full page yet I am just placing notes. I am typing this so that if someone comes along they can format this page properly.)

[![Pressure](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/6/6d/Pressure.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045627)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/6/6d/Pressure.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045627)

Overpressure:

Damage occurs immediately after effect takes place and in high amounts. There is no known equipment that can be worn to stop this effect. Can be negated by opening a hatch or door. Be warned this may fling you out said hatch/door due to pressure equating. If you are underwater, than it may be recommended to pump small increments of gas out of room to lower pressure.

[![Suffocation](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/b/b3/Suffocation.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045542)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/b/b3/Suffocation.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045542)

Suffocation:

Suffocation is caused in environments that lack oxygen, such being submerged in water, or areas that have been pumped free of oxygen. Damage occurs after a small timer runs out. Scuba gear can be used to give temporary oxygen to users for 2 minutes. Oxygen bottles can be used to restore breath until they run out.

[![Warmicon](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/5/56/Warmicon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045340)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/5/56/Warmicon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045340)

Warm:

This effect happens when you stand relatively close to a fire, flame or a heater. After a small time period, any freezing or wet effects will be negated. Effect does not damage any player or animal at time of writing.

[![Burningicon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/d/d7/Burningicon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045446)

Burning:

Occurs when standing too close to a fire. Damage occurs instantly and does high damage without firefighters suit. Firefighters suit reduces damage done by burning by 95% and allows you to put fires out quicker with an extinguisher.

[![Weticon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/9/90/Weticon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518045129)

Wet:

This effect is caused when a player is semi or fully submerged in water. It is unknown if other liquids cause this effect. After some time has passed the Freezing effect takes place. Effect does not damage any player or animal at time of writing. Effect can be negated by obtaining the Warm effect.

[![Freezingicon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/f/fb/Freezingicon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518044956)

[https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/File:Freezingicon.png](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/File:Freezingicon.png)

[![Freezing2icon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/5/5b/Freezing2icon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518051117)

Freezing:

Effect is caused in cold environments such as the Arctic Islands, or a little after the Wet effect sets in. A small timer pops up and after the time is up damage in medium amounts occurs. This effect can be negated by wearing a thermal suit or managing to get the Warm effect. When the timer runs out a new icon appears which is to the left of this.

[![Radiationicon](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/5/5d/Radiationicon.png/revision/latest?cb=20240518044823)

Radiation:

Occurs in areas that have been saturated in radiation. Damage is caused in small increments but effect lingers for a long period of time. The HUD fizzes and distorts the longer a player stays in the danger zone. Hazmat suits give a high resistance to radiation but does not prevent you from getting the effect.
