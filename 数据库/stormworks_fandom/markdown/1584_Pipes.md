## Properties

| mass 1 |

Standard pipes are used to transfer

[liquids and gases](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Fluids)

from one location to another and can be combined with

[pumps, valves, and other parts](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Fluids)

to create complex fluid transfer systems. Pipes do not store fluids but rather act as a pathway for fluid transfer between connected parts. Fluids will not transfer through open-ended pipes with no connections. To allow this, if desired, a part such as a

[fluid port](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Fluid_Port)

or

fluid exhaust

must be used.

There are several pipe variations with angles and intersections. All variations have a cost of $5 and a mass of 1.

- Pipe Straight
- Pipe Angle
- Pipe Angle Corner
- Pipe T-Piece
- Pipe T-Piece Corner
- Pipe Cross
- Pipe Cross Corner
- Pipe Omni (6-way)
