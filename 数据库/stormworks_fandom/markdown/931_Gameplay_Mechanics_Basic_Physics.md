## Physics Of Fluid

The Fluid Physics of Stormworks follows a simplified physics which seems unrealistic due to its physics property.

### Pressure and Flow

The flow and the pressure of stormworks fluid is based on pressure gradient, which means the fluid in higher pressure tank or room are tend to move to lower pressure area

### Diffusion

Fluids are miscible between gases and liquids, which means they will balance the concentration when two rooms or tanks are connected(without single flow components, e.g., fluid flow valve, electric pumps), no matter the pressure and its gradient. However, relief valves can limit the fluid diffusion. When connecting two liquid storage spaces with a liquid relief valve, the diffusion will only occur for liquid but not gas, the same as the gas relief valve

### Liquid

Significantly, liquids in stormworks are not compressible

## Physics Engine

Physics engine used: [Bullet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullet_(software))

The physics in this game tend to be worse than in other physics based games in alpha/beta/early access state [*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*]. Sometimes the behaviour of the engine is still not realistic (e.g. high forces of pivots turning the entire vehicle). The air drag in this game is much more than it is in real life, the air acts like a soup. Thus propellers and jets are more powerful. This also makes gliding difficult. Lift forces are barely noticable as the main force keeping flying objects up is their velocity.
