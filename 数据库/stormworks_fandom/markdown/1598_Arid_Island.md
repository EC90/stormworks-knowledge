# Arid Island

## Overview

The Arid Island is a large group of landmass situated in the south of the map in Stormworks available to players with the [Industrial Frontier DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Frontier_DLC). Often referred to as "Arid" by players. The Arid Island is part of the Industrial Frontier DLC. Arid Island contain numerous landmarks and structures, many connected by [rails](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Rails).

Arid Island is covered in [rails](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Rails), with comparatively fewer roads present. There are many major landmarks on the Arid Islands including a run down power plant, several airports/landing strips and a launch site for space vehicles.

The default starting location for players with the Industrial Frontier DLC is [North Meier Outpost](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/North_Meier_Outpost).
