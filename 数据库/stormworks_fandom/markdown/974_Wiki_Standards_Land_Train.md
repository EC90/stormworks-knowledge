## Standard: **Train**

Standard

LR1

[Create your badge here](https://sws.flaffipony.rocks?s=LR1%20%20)

This standard is a general overview and not very detailed; For a very detailed train standard see the [Stormworks Rail Vehicles Standards](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land/Train/SRC_Train_Standard).

### Rules of this standard

- [Parent standard rules apply](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land)
- Lights: White/yellow-white lights in the direction of movement, red lights in the opposite direction. OR: follow the [Stormworks Rail Vehicle Standards](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land/Train/SRC_Train_Standard).
- Headlights (small or large spotlights)
- Emergency brake (a toggle button that can be reached easily and will stop the train as fast as possible)
- Pilot seat with hotkeys **OR** normal seat with the most important controls reachable (throttle, brakes)

### Feature rules

- see [global feature rules](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Features)
- c this vehicle has connectors to connect it with other vehicles ([The type and position of these have been defined by the Stormworks Rail Vehicle Standards](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land/Train/SRC_Train_Standard))

### Size rules

- None - This standards has no size depending rules
