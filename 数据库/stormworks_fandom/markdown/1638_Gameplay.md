**Stormworks** is the game where you design and pilot your own sea-rescue service (military/industrial/space service with DLCs), in a rich and dramatic physics playground. Steer or fly your custom-designed, block based, programmable vehicles into fierce oceanic storms. Plan and execute thrilling rescues in a variety of challenging crisis scenarios.

## Gamemodes

Ever since release of Stormworks, the game had 3 gamemodes, those being:

1. [Career](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Career)

A gamemode focused on earning money via delivering containers, completing missions and locating crates, then buying various hangars and making better vehicles with it.

2. [Custom](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Custom)

A gamemode allowing player to set their own limitations whether that'll be limited/unlimited money, electricity, fuel and ammo, natural disasters spawned by the player or some other mechanic to aid or hinder their gameplay.

3. [Classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic)

A gamemode focused on earning money via delivering containers, completing missions and collecting crates, then buying various hangars and making better vehicles with it with additional "[Research](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Research)" mechanic that requires player to find crates and complete missions to receive "Research points" that can later be used to open a category of items.

## Vehicles

There are 4 main types of vehicles a player can build, these include:

1. Boats - Utility/Naval Vessels, Cruise Ships, etc.

2. Cars - Trucks, Tanks, Exploration Vehicles, etc.

3. Planes - Props/Turboprops, Jets, V/STOL Capable Aircrafts, etc.

4. Helicopters - Rescue Helicopters, Helitankers, Delivery Helicopters, etc.

Additional types of vehicles do exist but are either mostly used for decorations, Custom mode dependent, are "Proofs of concept" or are multi-vehicles - combination of 1-4 main types of vehicles with possible additions (For example: Crane Arms).

"Proof of concept" types of vehicles focus on showcasing capabilities of microcontrollers (For example: Trajectory Calculating HUDs/HMDs) or discovered glitches that can allow players achieve infinite electricity, high speeds, etc.
