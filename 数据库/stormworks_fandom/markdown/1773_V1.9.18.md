# V1.9.18

**V1.9.18** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on November 24th, 2023.

## V1.9.18

- Balance/Rework - Wide scale adjustments to fluid pressure and flow through doors
- Balance - Rebalanced water jet energy conversion to better match old behaviour
- Balance - Reduced air scoop reaction to wind and velocity
- Balance - [#23038](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/23038) Rebalanced engine cooling and heat
- Balance - [#22867](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22867) [#22872](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22872) Reduced large firebox and diesel burner coolant capacity and reworked heat transfer to be higher in larger boilers
- Fix - [#22752](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22752) Improved flow in restricted scenarios, apply flow limiting after resolving fluid islands
- Fix - Crash when attempting to offset the physics position of a group where not all vehicles were loaded
- Fix - Turbojets no longer apply force in space
- Fix - [#22917](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22917) Weapons AI autocannon tanks not firing
- Fix - [#22510](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22510) [#23023](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/23023) Updated weapons AI boats and fixed AI captain raycasts colliding with character physics
- Fix - [#22191](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/22191) Resolved default disaster siren not containing a battery (running out of electric)
- Fix - [#21163](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/21163) Fixed sharks dragging characters through thin walls
- Fix - [#20947](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/20947) Ordnance type default value
- Fix - [#23109](https://geometa.co.uk/support/stormworks/23109) Keep active block functionality for vehicle groups
- Fix - Flush addon script deferred events immediately before triggering initial autosave after onCreate
- (Some addon data based on onVehicleSpawn callbacks missing if load initial autosave of a game)
