The world of Stormworks is made up of islands and larger landmasses. The game world of Stormworks is made up of Tiles, which allow for the world to be produced from a seed via procedural generation. The smaller islands often fit into a single tile, while the larger landmasses such as the [Sawyer Islands](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Sawyer_Islands) are made up of multiple tiles. Without modification of the game itself, it is impossible for the tiles making up the larger landmasses to not be generated together. The tiles are generated at the beginning of a save and do not become altered over time.

Some Islands come with [Workbenches](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building#Workbenches), which allow for the construction of vehicles, assuming you have 'purchased' said island. Landmasses come with several sites or locations where workbenches can be utilized.

Other locations are generated around the world such as settlements, wind turbines, oil rigs. Some of these serve a purpose as they support key game mechanics, and others are purely for decoration.

## Landmasses

These landmasses are large islands or groups of islands that always appear in the same place in every game.

- **[Sawyer Islands](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Sawyer_Islands)**: a set of six islands forming a large landmass in the center of the map. It has roads, railways, and airstrips.
- **[Arctic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Arctic)**: a grouping of small islands to the far north of the map. It is surrounded by icebergs.
- **[Isle of Donkk](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Isle_of_Donkk)**: a chain of islands all connected by railway and road bridges situated to the west of the Sawyer Islands.
- **[Volcanic Chain](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Volcanic_Chain)**: a chain of volcanic islands to the south-west of the Sawyer Islands.
- **[Arid Island](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Arid_Island)**: a large landmass to the south of the map, made up of deserts and mountainous regions. It is the largest landmass in the game, and has many roads and railways. Its presence on the map requires the [Industrial Frontier DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Frontier_DLC) to be turned on.

## Outpost and Bases

These areas are places which the player can purchase and then use to create vehicles or structures. The majority of them can also be selected as a spawnpoint for the game.

- **[Coastguard Outpost Beginner](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Coastguard_Outpost_Beginner)** ($10 000): A small island with a small boathouse allowing for the creation of a small boat or submarine. It will always spawn in a pair with the St. Sebastiaz Hospital island. It is the location of the tutorial mission, that is started upon starting a game here.
- **[Coastguard Outpost TT](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Coastguard_Outpost_TT)** ($10 000): A small island with a small hangar and small helipad allowing for the creation of a small VTOL.
- **[Harbour Base](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Harbour_Base)** ($15 000): A small island with a medium-sized dock that allows for the creation of medium-sized boats. It is a natural upgrade to the Coastguard Outpost Beginner.
- **[Airstrip Base](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Airstrip_Base)** ($30 000): A small island with a small hangar and runway allowing for the creation of a small plane.
- **[Terminal Spycakes](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Terminal_Spycakes)** ($40 000): A harbor in the west of the Isle of Donkk allowing for the creation of a large boat, and trains. Named After The youtuber [Spycakes](https://www.youtube.com/spycakes)
- **Fishing Village** ($50 000): A small village in the south-east of the Sawyer Islands. It allows for the construction of a large boat. It also has a gantry that buys diesel.
- **Dreimor Airstrip** ($50 000): An airstrip in the west of the Isle of Donkk allowing for the creation of a large airplane.
- **[North Harbor](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/North_Harbor)** ($60,000): A coastal harbor on the north of the Sawyer Islands, with room for the construction of large boats, and trains.
- **Harrison Airbase** ($80 000): An airstrip with a hangar allowing for the construction of large planes.
- **Terminal Camodo** ($80 000): A harbor in the east of the Isle of Donkk allowing for the construction of a large boat and trains. Named After The Youtuber Named [Camodo Gaming](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCC6ulNJ-KDoIa3VI2gcvQpA)
- **Military Base** ($90 000): A large island with two runways, a large hangar, and a large dock. Large aircraft can be made here, but ships cannot.
- **Custom Base Submarine** ($100 000): A medium-sized island with a hidden cave, allowing for the creation of large submarines. It contains two base construction tables, one in the cave and one on top of the island.
- **Custom Base Heli** ($100 000): A small island with a flat top, allowing for the construction of a base. It does not have any vehicle construction tables.
- **Custom Base Large Boat** ($100 000): A medium-sized island with a base construction table, and space to build a large boat.
- **Custom Base Small Boat** ($100 000): A small island with a base construction table, and space to build a small boat.
- **Custom Base Oil Rig (Large)** ($100 000): A structure allowing for the construction of a custom oil rig. It has an oil deposit underneath the structure.
- **Terminal Endo** ($100 000): A rail yard in the south of the Arctic's main island, allowing for the construction of trains.
- **Terminal Trinite** ($100 000): A rail yard in the north of the Arctic's main island, allowing for the construction of trains.
- **O'Neill Airbase** ($120 000): An airstrip with a hangar allowing for the construction of large planes. It is located near to a train terminal that buys coal.
- **Coastguard Outpost** ($150 000): A large island with a large hangar, runway, and harbor. It's intended for use as a base of operations when playing in custom mode.
- **Tajin Airstrip** ($200 000): A large airstrip in the Arctic, allowing for the construction of airplanes.
- **Multiplayer Base** ($220 000): A large island with a large hangar, two runways, and a dock. The hangar and the dock both allow for the construction of two aircraft and boats at the same time.

The following bases are situated on the Arid Island, and can thus only be used if the [Industrial Frontier DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Frontier_DLC) is turned on.

- **North Meier Outpost** ($30 000): A small harbor in the north-west of the Arid Island, with a small dock and garage, allowing the construction of small boats, land vehicles, and VTOLs.
- **Brainz Train Yard** ($45 000): A train yard allowing for the construction of trains.
- **Uran Wind Power Plant** ($230 000): A big harbor in the north of the Arid Island, allowing for the construction of large boats.
- **Serpentine Train Yard** ($60 000): A train yard allowing for the construction of trains.
- **30 Dollar Train Yard** ($60 000): A train yard allowing for the construction of trains.
- **Mauve Train Yard** ($45 000): A train yard allowing for the construction of trains, near to the Lex Truck Stop.
- **JSI Dock** ($80 000): A medium-sized harbor with space to build two large boats.
- **FJ Warner Docks** ($80 000): A large harbor with a train yard and a hangar allowing the construction of large boats, planes, land vehicles, VTOLs and trains.
- **Clarke Airfield + Monkey Brain Launch Site** ($120 000): A large airfield with a large hangar, A large rocket launch site, allowing for the construction of rocket ships and large planes.
- **Endair Airfield** ($140 000): An airfield in the south-west of the Arid Island, with a hangar allowing the construction of airplanes.
