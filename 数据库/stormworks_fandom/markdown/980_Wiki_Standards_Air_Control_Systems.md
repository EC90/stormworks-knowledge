## Control Systems

There are two systems used in the workshop: Option A:

- WS Pitch
- AD Roll
- ↑ ↓ Throttle/Speed/Collective
- ← → Yaw

Option B:

- WS Throttle/Speed/Collective
- AD Yaw
- ↑ ↓ Pitch
- ← → Roll

Please add a toggle button to your vehicle that switches betwen those two modes (using numeric switchboxes). Set the default mode to whatever you prefer.
