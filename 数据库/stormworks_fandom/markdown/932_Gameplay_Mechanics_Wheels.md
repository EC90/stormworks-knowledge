## Wheels

There are two types of wheels in the game (historically):

- The old ones (Small Wheel & Medium Wheel) use different and more stable physics. They do not have any built in suspension.
- The new wheels offer wheels with different sizes, each with or without suspension. Sometimes the wheels move even if the brakes are applied, sometimes they do not provide thrust (e.g. when rotated by 90°) or create momentum that lifts your vehicle (sometimes when you place wheels upside down like a reserve wheel).
