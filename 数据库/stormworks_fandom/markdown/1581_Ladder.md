## Properties

| mass 3 |

> *"Single ladder rung. Multiple rungs can placed on top of each other to form a ladder of arbitrary length. You can attach to the ladder by interacting with [f], then detach again with either [f] or [space]. You will automatically detach from the ladder when you reach the top."*

Ladders can be built with a continuous straight line of ladder blocks. At least two ladder blocks are necessary to have a climbable ladder. Gaps of no more than one block (either empty space or other blocks) may be placed in between ladder blocks without impairing functionality.
