A Loot Crate can be found on islands and in the water in [Classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Gamemodes/Classic) game modes. Loot chests may also be found, as shown in the screenshot above.

Rewards include Cash and [Research](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Research) Points.

- Research points: 0-12 points
- Cash ranges: $0-10000
