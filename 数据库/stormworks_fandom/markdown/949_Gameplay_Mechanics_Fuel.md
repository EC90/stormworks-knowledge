# Gameplay/Mechanics/Fuel

Fuels are used in [engines](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Guides/Engine) to generate [mechanical power](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Mechanical_Power). There are four different types of fuel: diesel, jet fuel, coal, and nuclear fuel rods.

## Diesel

Diesel fuel is used by [Diesel Engines](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion/DieselEngines).

In [career](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Career) and [classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic) mode, vehicles spawn with the diesel provided at the spawn point. This means that you must manually replenish each spawn point, and by extension, any future vehicles you spawn.

Diesel can be purchased at gantries for 2$/l, and subsequently sold at other Diesel gantries for 3$/l.

## Jet fuel

Jet fuel is used by jet combustion chambers in [jet engines](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Jet_Engines).

In [career](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Career) and [classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic) mode, vehicles spawn with the jet fuel provided at the spawn point, and if it gets depleted it must be manually replenished, as with diesel fuel above.

Note that jet engines tend to use less fuel than diesel engines to generate the same force, though jet fuel is more expensive.

## Coal

Coal is used by [fireboxes](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Firebox) to heat [boilers](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Steam_boiler) in steam engines.

In [career](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Career) and [classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic) mode, each base starts off with 100 coal which will be placed into any vehicle that spawns with hoppers or ducts set to contain coal. Any additional coal to be used must first be mined using a mineral drill and then dumped into the base's coal hopper using a funnel duct. Alternatively, unused coal will also be returned to the base's storage when the vehicle is brought back to base and despawned.

Coal can also be sold for $2 per unit at either Terminal Camodo east of the Isle of Donkk, at the railway station near O'Neill Airbase in the northeast [Sawyer Islands](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Sawyer_Islands), or at Terminal Trinite in the arctic.
