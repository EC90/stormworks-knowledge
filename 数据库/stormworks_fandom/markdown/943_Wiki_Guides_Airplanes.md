## Airplanes

Airplanes generate lift by using [wings](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Vehicle_Control#Wing_Front_Section). More information on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lift_(force)).

You need to place the wings like this to create lift: [![Wing Lift](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/d/d0/Wing_Lift.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/150?cb=20190514095316)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/d/d0/Wing_Lift.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190514095316)

1. Learn about [control surfaces of airplanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_control_surfaces). 2. Choose a propulsion system: Propellers (powered by aircraft engines) for small and medium speed or jet engines for high speed

3. Always use a PID to control jet engines

4. Have a look at Aircraft and Helicopter Standards
