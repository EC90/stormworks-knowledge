# Wiki/Vehicles/Air/Helicopter

## Helicopter

[![Helicopter hovering with a harness lowered to the ground on a winch.](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/6/62/Screen_7.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/300?cb=20190327192237)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/stormworks_gamepedia_en/images/6/62/Screen_7.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20190327192237)

[https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/File:Screen_7.jpg](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/File:Screen_7.jpg)

Helicopter hovering with a harness lowered to the ground on a winch.

Helicopters are extremely useful in missions. They are faster than ships, can change altitude and can hover, allowing them to provide direct access to otherwise inhospitable environments for rescue. For some missions you may need to land on water, so floating designs can also be quite helpful ([Bouyancy](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Bouyancy)). Alternatively, if the weather is too bad to safely land on water, you can use a custom [microcontroller](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Workbench/Microcontrollers) to allow your helicopter to hover in one position. You can use a winch and [rope](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Rope) to get to/from the helicopter and rescue [survivors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor) without it ever needing to touch the water.

Helicopters generate lift by spinning [rotors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building/Components/Propulsion). Since rotors are spinning wings they create lift similar to normal wings. More information on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lift_(force)).
