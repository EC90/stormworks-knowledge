# Equipment

Stormworks has a variety of equipment, including outfits, tools, weapons, and gadgets with a wide variety of uses. Players have 8 small equipment slots, 1 large equipment slot, and 1 outfit slot. Carrying equipment reduces move speed in proportion to its weight.

A lot of equipment is battery-operated and can be recharged by putting it back into an equipment rack which has power available; some non-battery operated equipment also can be replenished when put into a powered equipment rack, like oxygen masks.

## Small Equipment

Small equipment fits into a 1x1 equipment rack. Players can carry eight items of small equipment at a time.

- **Flashlight:** Replaced the pre-1.0 Headlamp. Battery-powered, shines a cone of light ahead of you. Recharges slowly in a powered locker.
- **Binoculars:** Allows you to zoom in further. Doesn't use power or require charging.
- **First Aid Kit:** Heals you, other players, or NPCs. Maximum (also default) stack of 4, consumable. Each use restores about half of a full health bar for a player or NPC, or a full health bar for a [Stormwoof](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormwoof).
- **Night Vision Binoculars:** Battery-powered, allows you to zoom in with [night vision](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Infrared_mode). Does not allow regular vision.
- **Oxygen Mask:** Allows you to breathe in an environment without oxygen or excessive CO2, but much more limited capacity than SCUBA or diving gear. It offers 10 breaths, approximately enough for two minutes. Left click needed to breathe. Quickly refills when in a powered locker.
- **Compass:** Has two needles, the white points south, the red points north. Doesn't use power or require charging. Shows you what bearing you are facing in degrees shown above the compass' position in the equipment slot where it was placed.
- **Radio:** Battery-powered; can be used to chat with other players on the same channel (limited to channels 0-8) with a maximum effective range of 500 meters. Can also be used to signal vehicles with speakers (which produce an on/off output when receiving voice data from a linked radio channel), allowing for limited [radio](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Workbench/Components/Radio) control.
- **Remote Control Unit:** Battery-powered; allows you to remotely control a vehicle (as long as you set it up correctly). Limited to using radio channels 0-8, and an effective range of up to 500 meters.
- **Glowstick:** Provides hands-free illumination, works underwater. Lasts for somewhere between 25-30 minutes. Maximum (also default) stack of 12.
- **Flare:** Allows you to throw a road flare, provides very bright hands-free illumination, works underwater. Lasts for five minutes. Maximum (also default) stack of 4, consumable.
- **Flare Gun Ammo:** Maximum (also default) stack of 4. Used for reloading the Flare Gun. Can be refilled by unloading the Flare Gun, consumable.
- **Flare Gun:** Fires a flare in the direction you are facing (usually with an angle limit), contains one flare by default. Can be unloaded to refill Flare Gun Ammo (if you have it equipped).
- **Transponder:** Battery-powered, transmits a transponder signal, can be switched to MOB mode to activate automatically in water, has a battery meter
- **Strobe Light:** Battery-powered, model identical to transponder, flashes a strobe light when turned on, has a battery meter. Can be set to MOB (man overboard) mode like the transponder.
- **Strobe Light (IR):** Model identical to transponder, flashes a strobe light in [IR](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Infrared_mode) when turned on, has a battery meter. Can be set to MOB mode like the regular strobe light and transponder.
- **Radiation Detector:** A small battery-operated Geiger counter.

### Weapon DLC

The [Weapon DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_DLC) added:

- **Pistol:** A short-ranged semi-automatic pistol with a magazine capacity of 17 rounds.
- **C4 Explosive:** Can be placed on vehicles or terrain and remotely detonated.
- **C4 Detonator:** Remotely detonates any C4 placed by the player.
- **Hand Grenade:** Can be thrown a short distance - only accurate when standing still and throwing. Detonates after a short delay.
- **Pistol ammo:** An extra 17-round pistol magazine, Consumable.
- **SMG ammo:** An extra 40-round SMG magazine, Consumable.
- **Rifle ammo:** An extra 30-round rifle magazine, Consumable.
- **Speargun ammo:** Maximum(also default) stacked by 8 of speargun ammo, Consumable.

## Large Equipment

Large equipment fits in a 3x1 equipment rack. Players can only carry one item of large equipment at a time.

- **Fire Extinguisher:** Puts out fires. Carries a maximum of 9L of water and cannot be refilled.
- **Cable:** Can connect to two electric connectors. Useful for jump-starting a vehicle with a dead battery or for creating a wired data transfer link.
- **[Rope](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Rope):** Can connect two rope anchors to each other. Most commonly used for towing boats, cars, and trucks. Can also be used to winch a player into a helicopter while carrying a [survivor](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor), among many other possibilities.
- **Hose:** Can connect two liquid hose anchors to each other. Can also be used as a fire hose if it is a connected to a fluid nozzle with a pump supplying water.
- **Defibrillator:** Can revive incapacitated players and NPCs, has four charges, and can be recharged by placing in an equipment storage rack.
- **Welder:** Can fix components, cannot be used underwater, has 400 units of fuel, and cannot be refilled.
- **Underwater Welder:** Can fix components, works underwater, but has only 250 units of fuel compared to the welder and repairs slower.
- **Radio Signal Locator:** Battery powered; The name is deceptive. It does not detect radio signals, but will detect transponder signals and beeps faster as it gets closer to the source.
- **Fishing rod:** Used for catching fish which can be sold. A relaxing way to earn some extra money in [career](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Career) or [classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic) mode.

### Weapon DLC

The [Weapon DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_DLC) added:

- **Rifle:** An automatic rifle with a 30-round magazine. Accuracy is decent when firing single shots or short bursts, but very poor when firing continuously.
- **SMG:** A 40-round fully automatic submachine gun. Accuracy and range is poorer than with the rifle, but it is nonetheless effective in close quarters.
- **Speargun:** A single-shot weapon which works underwater.

## Outfits

Outfits fit in a 3x3 equipment rack. Players can only wear one outfit at a time.

- **Firefighting Gear:** Reduces fire damage by 95%.
- **Firefighting SCBA:** Reduces fire damage by 95% with a air tank, can be used in enclosed space for fire extinguishing, Slowly refills in a locker with oxygen connected
- **SCUBA Gear:** Allows you to breathe underwater for an extended period of time, increases dive depth, slowly refills in a locker with oxygen connected.
- **Diving Gear:** Better SCUBA gear with better pressure endurance, at the cost of slightly reduced visibility. Slowly refills in a locker with oxygen connected.
- **Parachute:** Allows slow falling by deploying when at high speed, can only be used once, though it repacks itself when placed in body equipment locker.
- **Arctic Gear:** Significantly slows the effects of low temperatures, such as in the Arctic or at very high altitudes.
- **HAZMAT Gear:** Reduces effects of radiation by 95%.
- **SPACE SUIT:** Provides O2 in space and has a temporary jetpack which uses up your O2, Slowly refills in a locker with oxygen connected
