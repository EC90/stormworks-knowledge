Trailers can be found at various industrial locations and freight terminals in Stormworks, and can be transported to other locations for varying amounts of money.

A blank built-in trailer is available in the presets.

## Design

Built-in trailers follow the same standard configuration as described below.

### Size

Trailers are 11 blocks wide x 17 blocks tall x 47 blocks long (2.75x4.25x11.75 m).

### Wheels

There are two pairs of double 5x5 wheels on both sides of the back of each trailer, for a total of eight wheels.

### Connectors

Trailers have an electric connector along the midline of the bottom surface near the front, 5 blocks away from being at the front edge. It is 5 blocks above ground level.

### Outriggers

There are two outriggers with robotic pivots to keep the trailer level when it is not being towed. These automatically pivot out of the way when the electric connector is connected.

### Logic

The trailer can be controlled through its electric connector. Numeric channel 1 controls the brakes. As for the boolean channels:

1. Left turn signal
2. Right turn signal
3. Brake lights
4. Reverse lights
5. Marker lights
