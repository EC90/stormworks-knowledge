Stormwoof is the term given to dogs in Stormworks, one of the animals added in the [Industrial Frontier DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Frontier_DLC). They can be somewhat helpful in rescue missions by barking in the direction of nearby survivors.

The dog whistle can be used to instruct a Stormwoof to stay (using a long burst), or to come to you (using two short bursts). A Stormwoof placed in a bed or seat will remain there until you move it elsewhere, even if you use two short whistle bursts.

Stormwoofs are guaranteed to spawn at [North Meier Outpost](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/North_Meier_Outpost) and FJ Warner Docks. There are a variety of breeds including the Beagle, Border Collie, Corgi, Daschund, Greyhound, Pug, Shiba, and Vizsla.
