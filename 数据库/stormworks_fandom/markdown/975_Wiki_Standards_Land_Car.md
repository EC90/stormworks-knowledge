## Standard: **Car**

Standard

LC1

[Create your badge here](https://sws.flaffipony.rocks?s=LC1%20%20)

### Rules of this standard

- [Parent standard rules apply](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Land)
- Pilot seat (either WS for throttle/brake and AD for steering OR use arrow UP DOWN for throttle/brake and arrow LEFT RIGHT for steering) **THIS RULE DOES NOT APPLY TO TRUCK TRAILERS OR CAR TRAILERS**
- Brake light (bright red light at the rear of the vehicle, can be the same light blocks used for normal rear light)
- Headlights (small or large spotlights) **THIS RULE DOES NOT APPLY TO TRUCK TRAILERS OR CAR TRAILERS**

### Feature rules

- see [global feature rules](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Standards/Features)
- c this vehicle has 1 horizontal electrical connector to connect it with trailers. Position of the block depends on the size (see below)

### Size rules

*The size does not influence the size of your vehicle! It just tells other people on which height your connector is*

- S connector height: 2
- M connector height: 3
- L connector height: 4
- XL connector height: 5
- XXL connector height: 6
