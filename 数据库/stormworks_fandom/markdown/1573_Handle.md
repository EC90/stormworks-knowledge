## Properties

| mass 1 |

> *"A handle that you can attach to by interacting with [q] or [e]. Interacting with [q] or [e] again will detach that hand. The handle can be used to drag vehicles around. If a vehicle is too heavy to move, you will be detached automatically when you move outside the handle's interaction range"*

Players can interact with this block to physically move it and anything attached to it, as long as the assembly is not too heavy[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation_needed)*].

Furthermore, the handle can also be used to get onto high surfaces. This is accomplished when a previously "too heavy" assembly is grabbed, using its handle, and the mouse is moved around. You can move your character by moving the mouse around, as if it was your hand. This means that when you move your mouse in a certain direction, your character moves in the opposite direction (eg. mouse down, character up)
