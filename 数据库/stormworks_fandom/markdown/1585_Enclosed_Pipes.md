## Properties

| mass 1 |

Enclosed pipes function the same as

[standard pipes](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Pipes)

with the only difference being their 1x1

[block](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Block)

form factor. This makes them useful to transfer fluids through sealed walls, to save space by hiding pipes inside structures, or simply for aesthetics. Enclosed pipes are not discernible from normal blocks unless the end is visible.

There are several pipe variations with angles and intersections. All variations have a cost of $5 and a mass of 1, which is the same as standard pipes.

- Pipe Straight (Enclosed)
- Pipe Angle (Enclosed)
- Pipe Angle Corner (Enclosed)
- Pipe T-Piece (Enclosed)
- Pipe T-Piece Corner (Enclosed)
- Pipe Cross (Enclosed)
- Pipe Cross Corner (Enclosed)
- Pipe Omni (Enclosed) (6-way)
