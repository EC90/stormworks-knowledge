# V1.9.8

**V1.9.8** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on October 29th, 2023.

## V1.9.8

- Rework - Reworked fluid system to be much more consistent with pre-space update behaviour. Fluid system now driven by delta pressure, and liquid is partially compressible to assist with flow. This update fixes steam, and other fluid component.
- Rework - Rebalanced RCS to have lower power and gas consumption

Fix - fixed exhaust and steam particles

Thanks to all the players on #experimental who helped prepare this update.
