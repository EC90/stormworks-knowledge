# Hospital ship

Hospital ships are mobile hospitals where [survivors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor) can be dropped off. They slowly patrol long ocean routes, stopping for about 10 seconds at a time after about every 1-1.5 km, as well as very infrequent stops (around once every half hour or so) of several minutes.

The back of the ship has a helipad suitable for landing a small [helicopter](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Guides/Helicopters), and there are [ladders](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Ladder) at the sides of the helipad going down to the water so players can climb up from a small boat. Rope anchors on both sides of the ladders are present to allow a small [boat](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Water/Boat) to be tethered to the ship, allowing a player to transfer survivors to the ship and return to their boat while both are in motion.

The ship has a med bay just in front of the helipad, with two doors leading into it. These are the only unlocked doors on the ship. Survivors who have been brought into the med bay will count as 'rescued'.

## Tethering

To tether a boat to a hospital ship, you'll need a [rope](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Rope) and a rope anchor. To be able to use the ship's ladders, the rope anchor should be positioned close to the center of your boat (or at least not too close to the front) so that you'll have an area of your boat where you are safe to stand next to the ship's ladder. If you use a rope anchor at the very front of your boat, your boat will be behind the ladder, so you'd have to jump and catch the ladder which is very likely to fail. If you fail to catch the ladder, you'll fall into the water, get dragged underneath and between the ship and your own boat and left stranded assuming you manage to avoid being killed by a propeller. Depending on how rough the weather is, you may or may not be able to swim fast enough to catch up and try again. So, don't place the rope anchor all the way at the front.

When you are ready to tether to the hospital ship, carefully adjust your throttle to very slightly exceed the ship's speed and angle your boat to slowly drift into it from the side. As you come up to the rope anchor, set your rope length to 2 meters and then quickly tether the ship and your boat together. Once tethered, you can cut your throttle to save fuel, trip your circuit breaker to save power, and then transfer all of your rescued survivors up to the ship's deck and into the med bay.

## Landing

Landing on the helipad with a small helicopter is not overly difficult as the helipad is fairly large and the ship remains remarkably stable even in rough weather. However, care must be taken when landing as the ship can stop unexpectedly.

For securing a helicopter to the pad (e.g. in case a tsunami is approaching), note that there are [small winches](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Small_winch) on both sides and one at the back. All the winches have ropes stowed next to them. This allows a player to attach the rope between the winch and a rope anchor on their helicopter, then winch it in to secure it. Be careful when removing the ropes - if you've winched the helicopter in tightly on all three winches and then suddenly detach one of the ropes, it can be yanked forcefully. Start by winching out a bit on all of the winches, then detach the ropes.
