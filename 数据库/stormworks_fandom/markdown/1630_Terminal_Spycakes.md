A harbor in the south west of the Isle of Donkk allowing for the creation of large boats, and trains. It was named after the YouTuber [Spycakes](https://www.youtube.com/spycakes). Stormworks base description: "Large dock and small village with various access routes to the island."

## Features

The site can be purchased for $40,000, granting access to its a harbor workbench for editing large ships of up to 56.75 m (227 blocks) L x 21.75m (87 blocks) W x 19.75 m (79 blocks) tall and a train station with four depots and a workbench in the center station. It has a Diesel Fuel, Jet Fuel, and Electricity anchors, alongside mineral storage. The island also has 4 beds and a wardrobe in the building next to the sheds.

It has a road connection to the Donkk Coal Mines about 500 m away where [coal](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Game_Mechanics/Fuel#Coal) can be mined, alongside several trailers that can be transported. Terminal Spycakes also has about a dozen [shipping containers](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Shipping_container) which can be transported to other train/harbor terminals for sums of money generally varying between $3,000 for short trips or up to $30,000+ for long trips, like the BVG Logistics depot. There is a diesel gantry close by in the village up the hill. It is also connected to the Dreimor Airstrip base which can be bought for $50,000.

With the [Weapons DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons_DLC), Terminal Spycakes also features an outpost control van. If the site is captured by the enemy, enemy vehicles will spawn at Terminal Spycakes until the player captures the site by clearing nearby enemies and staying in the van until the site reaches 100% captured. Note that the player can still purchase the harbor to spawn vehicles using the train or harbor workbench even if has been captured by the enemy; they just risk being shot at in the process.
