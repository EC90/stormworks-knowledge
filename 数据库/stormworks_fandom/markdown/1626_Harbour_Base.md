A small island with a medium-sized dock that allows for the creation of medium-sized boats. It is a natural upgrade to the Coastguard Outpost Beginner.

"Harbor Base, Large dock." Stormworks Base Description

## Features

The site can be purchased for $15,000, granting access to its a harbor workbench for editing large ships of up to 21.75 m (87 blocks) L x 56.75m (227 blocks) W x 19.75 m (79 blocks) tall. It has a Diesel Fuel, Jet Fuel, and Electricity anchors, alongside mineral storage. The island also has 1 bed and no wardrobe in the building with an attached garage.

This base does not spawn any trailers or containers to sell

With the [Weapons DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons_DLC), Harbour Base also features an outpost control van. If the site is captured by the enemy, enemy vehicles will spawn at Harbour Base until the player captures the site by clearing nearby enemies and staying in the van until the site reaches 100% captured. Note that the player can still purchase the harbor to spawn vehicles using the harbor workbench even if has been captured by the enemy; they just risk being shot at in the process.
