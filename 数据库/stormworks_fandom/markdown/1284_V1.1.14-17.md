**V1.1.14-17** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on March 26th, 2021.

- Rework - Vehicle bodies that don't generate buoyancy data properly will have drag applied

- Fix - Improved performance of AI aircraft add-on
- Fix - Stopped ai_debug command from causing issue in multiplayer
- Fix - Can now deliver survivors to the hospital correctly
