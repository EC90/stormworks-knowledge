# Gameplay/Research

Research is a feature that only exists in the [Classic](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Gamemodes/Classic) game mode.

Accessing the research tree is done through the map, default key: M

You earn research points by completing [missions](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Missions) and finding [loot crates](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Loot_Crate).

Prior to starting any research, you must have a sufficient amount of points and have satisfied any prerequisites. To start a research, press the blue research button near the date. The length of in-game time to research takes varies by research. As research uses in-game time, [sleeping](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Sleep) can be used to bypass the time.

*Information is current as of v1.10.9 - The Gas Spawner Update*
