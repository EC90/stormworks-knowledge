A small island with a helipad and a boat launch ramp, Ideal for VTOL, Helicopters, and craft that are deployed into water using trailers.

"Features a hanger and helipad." Stormworks Base Description

## Features

The site can be purchased for $10,000, granting access to its workbench for editing vehicles of up to *. It has a Diesel Fuel, Jet Fuel, and Electricity anchors, alongside coal storage. You are able to turn on and off the lights, as well as shut the shutters at the front of the hanger. The island also has 1 bed and no wardrobe in the Lighthouse.

This base does not spawn any trailers or containers to sell
