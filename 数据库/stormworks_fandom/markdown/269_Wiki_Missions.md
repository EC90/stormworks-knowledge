# Wiki/Missions

In *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)*, players can take their custom-designed [vehicles](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Vehicle) on various [missions](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Mission).

Missions range anywhere from basic deliveries, requiring only a simple creation, to perilous underwater ocean rescue missions, requiring advanced vehicles with logic systems. Missions take place at various locations both in the water and on land. The most common missions involve rescuing [survivors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor) by bringing them to a hospital.

### Procedural mission variants

- Repair a ship. Can be many different types of ships, including small yachts or large tug boats. Reward: $2,000.
- Lifeboat emergency. "A lifeboat in distress is requesting assistance" A [transponder](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Workbench/Components/Transponders) will be activated to help locate the lifeboat. A lifeboat may have one or more [survivors](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Survivor) who need to be rescued. Reward: $3,000 per survivor.
- Vehicle servicing: Involves repairing or towing a vehicle. Can be any of a variety of vehicles, including a small speedboat up to a cargo freighter. Can also involve towing land vehicles (e.g. a red cabrio) to other locations. With the Space DLC, it can even involve towing a lunar rover to a lunar base or repairing an air leak on a space station. (Reward = $2,000?)
- Tow an old sea mine to port: The mine is armed and has the potential to detonate before you even go near it.
- Fire: Can be an observatory fire, oil rig fire, wind farm fire, etc. $3,000 to put out the fire, then the usual $3,000 per survivor rescued.
- Save survivor from wild animal ([Industrial Frontier DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Frontier_DLC) + [Weapon DLC](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Weapon_DLC)): A survivor will need to be saved from some large wild animal, like a mountain lion or black bear. $2,000 for defeating the animal and $2,000 for getting the survivor to a hospital. About half of a rifle magazine is generally enough ammo to take the animal down.
- Transport: For example, transport a lighthouse keeper from an airport to a lighthouse. May include small luggage or boxes (note that you cannot carry things while seated). Variable rewards, around $2,000-3,000.
