North Meier Outpost is a small outpost in the north-west of the [Arid Island](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Arid_Island), 1.6 km southeast of Raptor Lighthouse and 600m NE of St. Alexander Hospital. It can be purchased for $30,000. There is also an oil deposit with a construction workbench that does not need to be purchased to be used about 2 km SE of the outpost.

For players with the Industrial Frontiers DLC, this is the default starter base for [classic mode](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Gameplay/Gamemodes/Classic).

## Features

It features a small dock and garage. The dock allows for construction of small boats, submarines, and amphibious vehicles up to 14.25x6.75x6.75m. The garage allows for construction of cars, trucks, and small helicopters or VTOLs up to 17.75m (71 blocks) long x 15.75m (63 blocks) wide x 5.25m (21 blocks) tall. A road leads from the garage out to the rest of the island.

Since [V1.6.2](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/V1.6.2), this location and FJ Warner Docks are the only locations guaranteed to spawn a Stormwoof (dog) which can assist the player in locating nearby survivors by barking in their direction.
