# V1.1.13

**V1.1.13** of *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)* was released on March 19th, 2021.

## V1.1.13

- Feature - AI planes - New AI and vehicle type to allow planes to roam the world of Stormworks. Addition of planes for a total of 21 plane and helicopter types.

- Rework - Minor improvements to LUA help page formatting

- Fix - Avoid helicopters grouping up at helipads
- Fix - Avoid helicopters and aircraft getting stuck on specific tasks
- Fix - Corrected move speed of aircraft when not in load range
- Fix - Reordered multiplayer data so most important vehicle information is sent first
- Fix - Fixed crash caused when vehicles occasionally fail to load
- Fix - Rotors now repair correctly when destroyed
- Fix - No longer reset AI state after loading scripts
