# Wiki/Vehicles

In *[Stormworks: Build and Rescue](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Stormworks:_Build_and_Rescue)*, players design or import **vehicles** to use in [missions](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Missions) for their rescue operations, including [planes](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Air/Plane), [helicopters](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Air/Helicopter), [boats](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Water/Boat),[submersibles](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Water/Submersible), and [trains](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Vehicles/Land/Train). Players can either use [vehicle workbenches](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Building#Workbenches) to design their own vehicles, or to spawn vehicles made by other players through the Steam Workshop ([learn more here](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Workshop)).

You can find out more about "*how to build vehicles*" in the [guide section](https://stormworks.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki/Guides).
