Description
This system draws Radar and Sonar detection on map, to easily locate any object in Stormworks.

Sonar is deprecated ATM, will soon update for new sonars

/!\ The HUD has been updated but it's as easy as always to understand

Features
  • Radar & Sonar on one display, with detection projected on a map for easier spotting.
  • Displays mass (M) and relative altitude (H) of the targets.
  • Displays if it's a known target by mass (shark, megalodon, kraken, whale, ...).
  • Complete touchscreen controls, intuitive UI.
  • Various map coloration theme settings to choose from.
  • Compatible with most monitors sizes.
  • Many customization options

Installation
Just copy-paste all the elements with the selection tool to where you want them to be on your vehicle.
Avoid rotating or flipping stuff like the radar or the compass sensore, since this can break the system.

Radars must be rotating clockwise and best ad 50% speed (the faster it is the more likely to miss detections).

/!\ Due to a game bug, the touchscreen composite data often disconnects itself while cut/paste, check that. /!\

Usage
Complete touchscreen controls
  • R: activate radar
  • S: activate sonar
  • Speaker logo: mute/unmute buzzer
  • +: zoom in
  • -: zoom out
  • M: switch between map coloration theme modes
  • Tergets: hold touchscreen on the green and blue dots to display relative altitude and mass (when avaiable).

Setup
The microprocessor offers various customisations:
  • Radar Range: change this according to what your radar estimated range is
  • Detection Filter: targets closer to this amount of meters will be ignored
  • Sonar: enable or disable sonar, useful to clean the UI when you just want the radar.
  • Sonar Type: choose between large and small sonar
  • Buzzer: choose if spawns muted by default or not
  • Display: set your monitor dimensions here
  • Map Color 1: choose your map coloration theme when in day mode
  • Map Color 2: choose your map coloration theme when in night mode
